Dynamic Wasteland turns Kenshi into a living, evolving world where nations act without waiting for the player. Wars erupt, cities are conquered, governments rise and fall, coups and civil wars reshape borders, religions spread, NPCs form personal relationships, leaders gain power, and every campaign creates its own emergent history.

DYNAMIC WASTELAND
A Living World Simulation Overhaul for Kenshi

"The world doesn't wait. It lives."

Dynamic Wasteland is an ambitious overhaul designed to transform Kenshi from a mostly player-reactive world into a living simulation that continues evolving with or without you.

Nations can go to war, cities can change hands, governments can rise and collapse, rulers can be overthrown, civil wars can divide entire states, religions can spread or fracture, alliances can form and break, crises can define entire campaign eras, NPCs can form personal relationships, rising heroes can appear in the wasteland, and political history can develop naturally over hundreds of in-game days.

You are not the center of the world.

You are simply another person trying to survive inside it.


DESCRIPTION

Vanilla Kenshi contains many world-state reactions, but most major political changes require specific player actions.

Dynamic Wasteland changes that philosophy.

The world now has its own political simulation.

A nation may declare war while you are mining copper on the other side of the continent.

A governor may rebel against their ruler.

A general may gain enough influence to launch a coup.

A city may declare independence and become a Free City.

A ruler may be deposed, imprisoned, executed or replaced.

A religious movement may spread across several settlements.

A civil war may divide a powerful nation into multiple competing governments.

A drought, plague, slave revolt or mercenary crisis may reshape an entire era.

You may participate in these events, influence them, exploit them...

or ignore them entirely.

The simulation continues either way.


NATIVE INDEPENDENT RUNTIME (v5.0)

Dynamic Wasteland now ships as a fully independent native mod.

• No KenshiLib required
• No RE_Kenshi required
• Own launcher: DynamicWastelandLauncher.exe
• Own runtime and ABI
• DW-owned per-slot save state (does not inject blobs into Kenshi quick.save)

You can still install RE_Kenshi for other mods if you want.
Dynamic Wasteland does not depend on it.


MAIN FEATURES

DYNAMIC WORLD SIMULATION

The world progresses independently from the player.

Major factions maintain strategic values such as:

• Military strength
• Economy
• Stability
• Legitimacy
• Political authority
• War exhaustion
• Aggression
• Government structure
• Controlled settlements
• Food / population pressure
• Diplomatic reputation

Strategic systems update over time without needing direct player involvement.


DYNAMIC WARS

Nations can evaluate other factions and independently decide whether war is worthwhile.

War decisions can consider:

• Relations
• Military strength
• Political stability
• Existing conflicts
• War exhaustion
• Strategic opportunities
• Rivalries
• Alliances and coalitions

Wars are not intended to happen randomly every few days.

Large geopolitical events are deliberately uncommon so the world remains recognizable even during very long campaigns.


CITY CONQUEST

Settlements can change political ownership as wars progress.

A conquered settlement may retain:

• Former owner
• Occupation status
• Resistance
• Governor
• Autonomy
• Integration state

Recently conquered territory may remain unstable and can become the source of future rebellions.


DIPLOMACY, ALLIANCES AND ULTIMATA

Nations can form deeper diplomatic relationships beyond simple hostility.

Supported concepts include:

• Bilateral alliances with mutual defense
• Coalitions that may join wars
• Ultimata with deadlines (tribute or peace demands)
• Truces and treaties
• Diplomatic reputation costs for betrayal

Breaking alliances and ignoring ultimata can reshape legitimacy and future wars.


GOVERNMENTS

Nations are no longer represented only as factions.

They can possess political governments.

Supported government concepts include:

• Monarchies
• Republics
• Free Cities
• Oligarchies
• Theocracies
• Military governments
• Dictatorships
• Federations
• Confederations
• City Leagues
• Tribal governments
• Revolutionary governments
• Warlord states

Government type can influence succession, legitimacy, governors, political authority, coups and civil wars.


LEADERS, DYNASTIES AND GOVERNORS

A national ruler does not personally administer every settlement.

Individual cities can have their own governors.

Ruling houses can develop dynasties with heirs, consorts, children and pretenders.

Governors and characters may have attributes such as:

• Loyalty
• Ambition
• Influence
• Popularity
• Competence
• Corruption
• Prestige
• Military support

A loyal governor may protect the state.

An ambitious governor may eventually become one of its greatest threats.

Diplomatic marriages and dynastic claims can create long-term political pressure.


COUPS

Political instability can create opportunities for coups.

Generals, governors and other important characters may challenge the existing government.

Possible outcomes include:

• Coup defeated
• Conspirators arrested
• Political executions
• Exile
• Government takeover
• Military junta
• Civil war
• New ruler


CIVIL WARS

Internal conflict can divide a nation.

Civil wars can emerge from:

• Failed coups
• Disputed succession
• Rebellious governors
• Religious conflicts
• Political instability
• Military defeat
• Separatism

Large nations may divide into two or even three competing political entities.

Each side can possess its own:

• Leader
• Cities
• Military strength
• Government
• Political objectives


RELIGION

Dynamic Wasteland includes a strategic religion system.

Religions can influence:

• Characters
• Cities
• Governments
• Politics
• Diplomacy
• Revolts
• Legitimacy

Religious movements can gain or lose followers over time.

The system supports concepts such as:

• State religions
• Religious authority
• Religious minorities
• Conversion
• Religious leaders
• Heresies
• Schisms
• Religious revolts
• Religious politics
• Player-founded religions


CRISES AND CAMPAIGN ERAS

The wasteland can enter named campaign eras driven by world crises.

Examples include:

• Drought
• Plague
• Migration / exodus
• Trade boom
• Mine collapse
• Slave revolt
• Beast invasion
• Ruin discovery
• Charismatic tribune
• Black famine

Only one major crisis is active at a time.
Each crisis can leave lasting marks on nations, cities and the chronicle.


GUILDS AND ORGANISATIONS

Nations can host guild chapters that influence wealth, corruption, crafts and legitimacy.

Examples:

• Merchants
• Miners
• Thieves
• Bounty hunters
• Assassins
• Artisans
• Religious orders

Guilds can enrich treasuries, raise unrest, support armies or undermine weak courts.


MERCENARIES

Independent mercenary companies can be hired by nations or the player.

Companies have captains, strength and wages.

Unpaid companies may desert or switch sides during war.

A player company can be funded, signed or broken through the F4 panel.


RISING HEROES

Unknown champions can emerge during long campaigns.

Four rising heroes (Balduin, Bjorn, Ricard and Jorn) can appear with:

• News and rumours
• Ambitions
• Regions of activity
• Optional FCS squad packs
• Political rise over time

They are not guaranteed early-game protagonists.
They arrive when the campaign is ready for them.


PERSONAL NPC RELATIONSHIPS

Faction reputation and personal relationships are separate systems.

You may be an enemy of a nation while an individual NPC within that nation personally likes or respects you.

Likewise, a member of your own faction may personally dislike you.

Character relationships can track concepts including:

• Opinion
• Trust
• Respect
• Affection
• Loyalty
• Rivalry
• Fear
• Attraction
• Jealousy
• Intimacy

Important NPCs can also form relationships with each other.

Friendships, rivalries and personal loyalties can influence political events such as coups and civil wars.


ROMANCE AND SOCIAL RELATIONSHIPS

Adult characters can potentially develop deeper personal relationships.

Depending on compatibility and circumstances, relationships may evolve through states such as:

• Friends
• Close friends
• Rivals
• Romantic interest
• Dating
• Partners
• Marriage
• Former partners
• Secret relationships

NPC autonomy remains important.

Relationships are not guaranteed simply because the player repeatedly selects a social interaction.


MILITARY CAREER AND PLAYER POWER

The player can pursue a military career within participating nations.

Possible progression:

Recruit
Soldier
Veteran
Sergeant
Captain
Commander
General

Military service can provide:

• Missions
• Merit
• Political influence
• Promotions
• Connections with commanders
• Opportunities to influence national politics

A sufficiently powerful general may eventually attempt to seize control of the government.

Players who become governors or rulers can also use the Power tab for realm actions such as:

• Appointing governors
• Declaring war or peace
• Managing capital and succession
• Diplomatic actions
• Guild / mercenary patronage


WORLD NEWS AND HISTORY

Major events are recorded by the Dynamic Wasteland news and chronicle systems.

Examples:

• Wars declared
• Cities captured
• Battles
• Coups
• Civil wars
• Elections
• Leadership changes
• Religious changes
• Revolutions
• Political marriages
• Independence declarations
• Crises and era beginnings
• Rising heroes

Open F4 → History to read:

• Nation history
• Wasteland chronicle
• Current era
• Rising heroes status

Over time, your campaign develops its own unique political history.


POLITICAL MAP

Press M to open the political map overlay.

Depending on mode, the map can visualize:

• Nation ownership
• Capitals and cities
• Wars
• Alliances
• Truces
• Ultimata
• Diplomacy focus
• Related political information

The map is intended as a visual representation of the history created by the simulation.


F4 WORLD PANEL

Press F4 to open the Dynamic Wasteland interface.

Tabs include:

• World
• Government
• Religion
• Map
• Social
• History
• Power
• Options
• Debug (when enabled)

Use Options to toggle major systems without reinstalling.


EXISTING SAVE SUPPORT

Dynamic Wasteland is designed to support existing saves.

When first installed into an existing campaign, the mod initializes its simulation using the current state of your world.

It does not require you to begin from Day 1.

IMPORTANT:

Dynamic Wasteland is a technically ambitious native plugin.

Always create a backup of important saves before installing a new major version.


INSTALLATION

1. Close Kenshi completely.
2. Download and extract this package.
3. Copy the folder DynamicWasteland into:

  Kenshi\mods\

  Final structure should look like:

  Kenshi\mods\DynamicWasteland\
      DynamicWasteland.mod
      DynamicWasteland.dll
      DynamicWasteland.ini
      Heroes.ini

4. (Optional) Copy DynamicWasteland_RisingHeroes into Kenshi\mods\ if you want Rising Heroes squads.
5. Copy DynamicWastelandLauncher.exe into the Kenshi root folder, next to kenshi_x64.exe.
6. Open the normal Kenshi launcher once and enable:
  • DynamicWasteland
  • DynamicWasteland_RisingHeroes (if installed)
7. Start the game with DynamicWastelandLauncher.exe
  (recommended for the native DW runtime).
8. Load your save or begin a new game.
9. Open the Dynamic Wasteland interface with F4.
10. Open the political map with M.

Custom Kenshi path example:

DynamicWastelandLauncher.exe "D:\Games\Kenshi"


REQUIREMENTS

• Kenshi (64-bit)
• DynamicWastelandLauncher.exe (included)
• No KenshiLib
• No RE_Kenshi

RE_Kenshi may still be installed for other mods.
It is not required by Dynamic Wasteland.


COMPATIBILITY

Dynamic Wasteland attempts to discover factions and settlements dynamically rather than relying exclusively on hardcoded vanilla factions.

The long-term goal is compatibility with:

• Vanilla Kenshi
• Modded factions
• Modded settlements
• World-state mods
• Large overhauls where technically possible

However, mods that heavily modify faction ownership, world states, town overrides or native systems may require compatibility patches.

Please report compatibility problems on the project Discord or Nexus bug-report section.


PERFORMANCE

Kenshi is already CPU intensive.

Dynamic Wasteland therefore avoids running the entire political simulation every frame.

Major systems operate using strategic ticks, caching and event-driven updates wherever possible.

CrashGuard can isolate failing modules so one fault does not always take down the whole game.

The goal is to create a deeper simulation without turning every rendered frame into a world-simulation calculation.


IMPORTANT NOTICE

Dynamic Wasteland is under active development.

This project interacts with systems that normal FCS mods rarely touch.

Major updates may introduce bugs.

PROJECT PHILOSOPHY

The world exists without you.

Kings can fall without meeting you.

Wars can begin without your permission.

Cities can change governments while you are travelling.

Friends can become enemies.

Generals can become rulers.

Religions can rise and collapse.

Entire states can disappear.

You can influence history.

But history does not wait for you.
